# Help Center: Example Online Store (fictional sample data)

This document is fictional sample content written for a demo. It does not describe any real business.

## Shipping

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days and is free for orders over $50. Orders under $50 pay a flat shipping fee of $5.99.

Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs $14.99 for any order size. Orders placed before 2 PM local time on a business day ship the same day.

We currently ship to the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Australia and Singapore. International orders may be subject to import duties, which are paid by the customer on delivery.

## Returns and refunds

You can return most items within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unused and in the original packaging. To start a return, open the order page, click "Request a return" and print the prepaid return label.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days after the warehouse receives the item. Shipping fees are not refunded unless the item arrived damaged or the wrong item was sent.

Final-sale items, gift cards and personalized products cannot be returned.

## Warranty

All electronics come with a 12-month limited warranty that covers manufacturing defects. The warranty does not cover accidental damage, water damage or normal wear. To make a warranty claim, contact support with your order number and a short video showing the problem.

## Payments

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal and Apple Pay. Payments are processed securely and card numbers are never stored on our servers. Orders are charged when they ship, not when they are placed.

## Account and orders

You can change the shipping address or cancel an order within 1 hour of placing it, from the order page. After that, the order is sent to the warehouse and can no longer be changed; you can return it after delivery instead.

To reset your password, click "Forgot password" on the sign-in page. A reset link valid for 30 minutes is sent to your email address.

## Contact support

Support is available by live chat and email, Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM. The average first response time by email is under 4 hours during business hours.
