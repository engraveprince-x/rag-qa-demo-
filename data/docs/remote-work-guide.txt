Remote Work Guide (fictional sample policy for demo purposes)

Working hours
Team members choose their own working hours but should be reachable during the core collaboration window of 10 AM to 3 PM in their team's primary time zone. Calendars must be kept up to date so colleagues can see when you are available.

Communication
Use the team chat for quick questions and the project tracker for anything that needs a decision or follow-up. Messages that are not urgent should not expect a reply within the same hour. For urgent production issues, page the on-call engineer instead of sending a chat message.

Meetings
Every meeting needs an agenda shared at least one day in advance. Meetings should be 25 or 50 minutes long to leave a short break. Notes and decisions are written in the shared document linked from the calendar invite.

Equipment
New team members receive a one-time home office budget of $800 for a chair, desk, monitor or other equipment. Laptops are provided by the company and must have disk encryption and automatic updates enabled.

Security
Never share passwords in chat or email; use the password manager. Enable two-factor authentication on every work account. Report a lost or stolen device to the IT team within 24 hours.

Time off
Team members get 20 days of paid time off per year plus local public holidays. Requests for more than 3 consecutive days should be submitted at least two weeks in advance.
